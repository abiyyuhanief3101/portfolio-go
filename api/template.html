The journey

A CS grad who almost quit tech to serve the grassroots.

01 The paradox

Computer Science at Universitas Indonesia — then a deep dive into community programs across Java and Sumatra. Technology felt disconnected from real human problems. So I went to find where the problems actually lived.

02 The revelation

A simple Django app reduced order processing from 5 minutes to under 1 minute for a local café, saving a family 1 hour every single day. That's when everything clicked. Tech isn't just code — it's empowerment at human scale.

“I almost quit tech. But then I saw how a simple system could improve financial status and save a family business 1 hour every day.”

Serving communities across the archipelago taught me that impact starts with empathy, not just coding. I'm back in the industry now — using Go and Python to build robust systems, with grassroots empathy baked into every decision.
