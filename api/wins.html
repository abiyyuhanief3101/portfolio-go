LAKSA Bogor

The official tourism information site for the Bogor City Tourism & Culture Office. The city's tourism information lived inside a chatbot iframe that Google could not index. In three phases over 14 days I took search-indexable pages from 0 to 76, built a back office where staff manage 55 destinations themselves, and added a directory of 250 official health facilities.
