About

Hi, I'm Abi.

Product Implementation Specialist at Nusatek. Information Systems graduate of Universitas Indonesia. I build things to solve problems — whether that's a full-stack system or a community program from scratch.

My background sits at the intersection of technology and people. I studied Information Systems at Universitas Indonesia, but the real education happened in the field: distributing IDR 200 million in business grants to 70 local entrepreneurs in North Sumatra, architecting a helpdesk ecosystem for a school management platform serving 2,800+ students, and running fundraising campaigns that reached hundreds of thousands of people online.

I believe the best solutions come from understanding the human problem first — then deciding whether the answer is a line of code, a program design, or both.
