The approach

Two solution paths, two kinds of business

Not every business needs the same build. Across the projects I've shipped, the requests sort themselves into two paths with opposite architectures — one built to stay accurate under daily pressure, the other built to be found and remembered. Most people already know which one they are by the second paragraph below.

Path 01 The Kitchen

Business Operations

For the engine room your business actually runs on.

Built for

  • Cafés, retail stores, small production houses
  • In-house cashier & order entry
  • Multi-location stock & warehouse control
  • Daily financial reporting dashboards

What success looks like

  • Transactions 45% faster than the manual flow it replaces
  • Manual logging errors designed out, not trained around
  • Sensitive financial data never leaves your premises
  • End-of-day reconciliation in a single click

Server cost: Rp 0/month — the system runs on your own device, and keeps running when the internet doesn't.

Golang · SQLite · Cloudflare Tunnel
